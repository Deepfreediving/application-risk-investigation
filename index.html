Who this is for

  • Founders who paid $5,000–$20,000 for an application and are about to open it to paying customers.
  • Anyone who has taken over a codebase from a contractor and has no way to tell what state it is in.
  • Non-technical owners who need a straight answer rather than a lecture about architecture.

This is not for developers who can simply run their own scanners — you will get more value from those tools than from this report.

What you receive

  • A written report that opens in any browser and prints to PDF.
  • Findings ranked by severity, each one citing the exact file and line that triggered it, so your developer can verify every claim in seconds.
  • The specific issues that cause real damage: credentials committed into the source code, licence obligations that could force you to publish your own product's code, dangerous handling of user input, and unlocked-down transport settings.
  • A plain-English bottom line, because you are not the engineer.
  • A prioritised list your developer can act on immediately.

What this is not

  • It is not a penetration test. Nobody attacks your running application.
  • It is not a guarantee that your application is safe. It reports only what the checks in scope can prove. Finding nothing is not proof that nothing is wrong.
  • It does not execute your application, so it cannot find runtime or business-logic faults.
  • Credential values found are redacted in the report and reported to you privately. A credential is never reproduced in the deliverable.
